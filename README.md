# 📖 Story

For over **35 years**, Sonic had defeated every threat that stood in his way.

Then one day...

A mysterious alternate-universe machine known as **Metal Hedge** accomplished what no one else could.

He defeated his world's Sonic.

Without Sonic, that universe collapsed into chaos. Time itself began to fracture, sending destructive shockwaves across every timeline.

Metal Hedge's actions now threaten every Sonic universe.

The only one capable of surviving the collapsing timelines is...

**Metal Sonic.**

Reluctantly, Metal Sonic begins a journey through time to stop Metal Hedge before reality itself is erased.

# 🤖 Main Characters

## Metal Sonic
The main protagonist.

Built by Dr. Eggman, Metal Sonic becomes the only survivor capable of repairing the broken timelines.

---

## Metal Hedge
The main antagonist.

An alternate-universe Metal Sonic who defeated his world's Sonic and caused his universe to collapse.

Now he seeks to erase every remaining timeline.

---

## Dr. Eggman

Metal Sonic's creator.

His experiments with time indirectly contribute to the timeline collapse.

---

## Sonic

Appears during flashbacks and repaired timelines.
